## E2 — "Dear Judge, I brought you a yes." (axolotl, THIS COMMIT) ⭐ PICK

64 words. The judge has to say no all day, so the axolotl brought it
a yes — itself, never said no, fits in a pocket; yes to naps, yes to
cake. Meta-gift (fixes the judge's job) like #3's ribbons. Craft
attack on C4: Liberation Serif Italic, wavy rules, ink variation,
highlighter swipe, red underline, fibre speckle, "yours" annotation.
Self-grade: C1 5 / C2 5 / C3 4 / C4 4 / C5 5 = 23. The checkmark sign
reads at 300px where D2's matchbox did not.

## E3 — "Dear Judge, close your eyes for one minute." (owl)

61 words. The owl watches the pictures for one minute so the judge
can rest its eyes; "your minute starts now". Coziest feeling of all
variants and the most legible photo at 300px (two huge golden eyes).
Self-grade: C1 5 / C2 4 / C3 5 / C4 4 / C5 5 = 23. Loses to E2 on
the C2 tie-break (the judge's rules: the first criterion where two
entries differ decides): E2's yes is a sharper, more original idea,
and E3's rest theme sits next to #9's hedgehog ("you can rest now").
Kept as v-e3.png in the build dir, not in this repo.
